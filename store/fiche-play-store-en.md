## Full description (4000 characters max)

FRONTIÈRES: ARKEN is a turn-based strategy game for your phone. Five nations share an archipelago of 20 provinces. You lead one of them: make it prosper, defend it and push its borders, by force or by words.

ONE GAME, MANY CHOICES
• Pick your nation among five: the Kingdom of Valmorne, the Confederation of Roc-Épine, the Empire of Noireflamme, the League of Oroël and the Alliance of Solmara
• Every leader's orders are played at the same time each turn
• A game lasts 80 turns at most: play at your own pace and resume your game where you left it
• No countdown: the game waits until you are done thinking

ARMIES, SEA AND FORTRESSES
• Recruit infantry, cavalry, armor, artillery and warships
• Build banks, factories, ports and fortresses to feed your economy and protect your provinces
• Cross the seas along sea routes, land on enemy coasts and have your fleet bombard coastal fortresses
• Fog of war hides enemy movements: anticipate them

LIVELY DIPLOMACY
• Talk with the leaders of the other nations: each has a character and grudges
• Offer a ceasefire, a pact or an alliance, ask your allies for help, or betray them if it suits you
• An ally can let your armies cross its territory

BOOSTS AND DAILY MISSIONS
• Earn tokens by completing daily missions, then spend them on five boosts: gold, recruitment, construction, defense and navy
• Each boost level gives +1%, up to +10%, and only to your nation: the AI never benefits from them
• An optional 8-turn tutorial teaches the basics and can be replayed at any time

FREE, NO TRAPS
The game is free. An ad may appear when you start a new game, never while you are playing and never in the tutorial. An optional ad can also earn you a token for a mission. If you close one early, no further ad is forced on you. You can manage your ad privacy choices in the settings.
The Starter Pack (one-time purchase) removes the ads shown at the start of a game and gives 3 tokens. Token bundles are also available. You can never buy gold or troops: the game stays about strategy, not about your wallet.
An internet connection is only needed for ads and purchases.
